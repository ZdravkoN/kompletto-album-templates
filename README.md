# Kompletto Album Templates

A collection of ready-made album templates for **[Kompletto: Sticker Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kompletto-sticker-tracker/id6771506154)** — the smart companion app for sticker and trading card collectors.

## About Kompletto

Kompletto lets you **collect, track & swap stickers** with ease. Point your camera at a sticker to automatically read its number and catalog it instantly. The app gives you real-time visibility into what you own, what you're missing, and what you have to trade — then helps you connect with other collectors to complete your collection.

**Key features:**
- Camera scanning to auto-detect sticker numbers
- Track owned stickers, missing pieces, and duplicates
- Match your missing list against others' duplicates for easy swaps
- Share your lists via AirDrop or messaging
- iCloud sync with no account or data collection required
- Supports numbers, letters, ranges, and special editions

Available free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kompletto-sticker-tracker/id6771506154) for iOS.
